# Quests and Encounters · Season One: Before the Snow

A playable browser demo, in English, Russian and Spanish. Raise walls and a watchtower at Ravenwick before the first snow comes down
out of the Greyspires. Best with sound on; click Begin to start.

Effects use [pixelfx](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx) (MIT); see `pixelfx/NOTICE.md`.

## Season Two: The Long Night

Play it at `season2.html`. Turn Ravenwick into a City before the Host comes back at Midwinter: a morning council,
three roads of new places, five districts to choose from, and a three-night siege. English only for now. It carries
on from your Season 1 chronicle if this browser has one, or starts with a default Company.
